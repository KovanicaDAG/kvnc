kvnc je nezavisni from-scratch Rust L1:
- Consensus: Mysticeti-style uncertified DAG (wave = 3)
- Execution: Wasmi (deterministički WASM)
- Account + staking model, 15–21 validatori, min stake 50k KVNC
- Resource target: 2–4 GB RAM
- Token: KVNC 9 decimals

5. Poštuj postojeće crate-ove (kvnc-types, kvnc-crypto, kvnc-storage, kvnc-dag, kvnc-consensus, kvnc-mempool, kvnc-network, kvnc-runtime, kvnc-execution, kvnc-staking, kvnc-node, kvnc-cli, kvnc-rpc).
