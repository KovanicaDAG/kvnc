# Kovanica Discord Server — V2

Launch-ready server layout with copy-ready channel topics. V2 keeps the channel list focused, routes repeatable questions into threads, and makes official information and safety guidance easy to find.

## 1. START HERE

### `#welcome`
**Topic to paste:**
> Welcome to the Kovanica community. Read `#rules-and-safety`, choose any optional roles in `#roles`, then introduce yourself or head to a channel that fits. Official links: [website] · [docs] · [explorer] · [GitHub].

### `#announcements`
**Topic to paste:**
> Official Kovanica announcements: project updates, releases, and event notices. Read-only. Follow linked sources for full details; beware of unofficial accounts and impersonators.

### `#rules-and-safety`
**Topic to paste:**
> Be respectful, keep discussion constructive, and report scams or abuse to moderators. Never share your seed phrase, private key, password, or recovery codes. Staff will never ask for them or DM you first to offer support.

### `#roles`
**Topic to paste:**
> Choose optional interest, language, and notification roles. Roles help organize conversations and pings; they do not indicate official status, investment access, or project authority.

### `#official-links`
**Topic to paste:**
> Canonical Kovanica links: [website] · [documentation] · [explorer] · [GitHub] · [status page]. Verify links here before connecting a wallet or downloading software. Report suspicious links to moderators.

## 2. COMMUNITY

### `#general`
**Topic to paste:**
> General Kovanica conversation. Be welcoming, stay on topic, and use a thread for long discussions. Never post private keys, seed phrases, or sensitive personal information.

### `#introductions`
**Topic to paste:**
> Say hello if you’d like: what brought you to Kovanica, and what are you interested in? Keep personal information private; introductions are optional.

### `#community-showcase`
**Topic to paste:**
> Share community projects, tutorials, artwork, and demos related to Kovanica. Include a short description and useful links. Be transparent about affiliations; no scams, spam, or unsupported price claims.

### `#off-topic`
**Topic to paste:**
> Casual conversation that isn’t directly about Kovanica. Keep it friendly and follow the server rules.

### `#events`
**Topic to paste:**
> Community calls, builder sessions, and project events. Check the host, date, and time zone before joining. Official Kovanica events will be clearly identified.

## 3. NETWORK & TESTNET

### `#network-status`
**Topic to paste:**
> Read-only network and testnet status updates. Confirm current details using the linked official status or explorer source. Testnet activity and balances are not a promise of mainnet availability or monetary value.

### `#testnet-help` — Forum channel
**Topic to paste:**
> Get help with Kovanica testnet, wallets, and transactions. Start one post per issue; include steps, software version, and redacted error details. Never share keys, seed phrases, passwords, or recovery codes.

### `#node-operators`
**Topic to paste:**
> Node setup and operator discussion. Share sanitized configuration and public logs only. Protect your keys and data directory; verify peer and network details against current official documentation.

### `#network-feedback` — Forum channel
**Topic to paste:**
> Report reproducible network or testnet issues and suggest improvements. One post per issue; describe expected vs. actual behavior, reproduction steps, and impact. Remove secrets and private user data.

## 4. BUILDERS

### `#builder-chat`
**Topic to paste:**
> Technical discussion for people building with or contributing to Kovanica. Share enough context to make questions answerable; use threads to keep longer topics organized.

### `#api-and-sdk`
**Topic to paste:**
> Discuss Kovanica APIs, SDKs, and integrations. Include the language, library or endpoint, and a minimal example when useful. Redact credentials and never share signing keys or seed phrases.

### `#protocol-discussion`
**Topic to paste:**
> Discuss protocol behavior and technical design. Distinguish shipped rules from proposals, link relevant documentation or code, and avoid presenting unapproved changes as committed roadmap items.

### `#documentation`
**Topic to paste:**
> Ask questions about Kovanica documentation or suggest corrections. Include the page link, the unclear or incorrect section, and a proposed clarification when possible.

### `#show-your-build`
**Topic to paste:**
> Demo what you’re building with Kovanica and ask for feedback. Explain what works today and link a repository or demo. Do not imply official endorsement without authorization.

## 5. PROJECT UPDATES & PROPOSALS

### `#release-notes`
**Topic to paste:**
> Shipped changes and release notices. Use linked release notes as the authoritative source for versions, upgrade steps, and compatibility details.

### `#roadmap`
**Topic to paste:**
> Project milestone updates and progress. Roadmap items are plans, not guarantees. Check linked project sources for current scope, status, and decisions.

### `#proposals` — Forum channel
**Topic to paste:**
> Propose and discuss improvements. One proposal per post; explain the problem, suggested change, alternatives, impact, and open questions. Label ideas as discussion—not official decisions—unless formally confirmed.

## 6. SUPPORT & SECURITY

### `#help-desk` — Forum channel
**Topic to paste:**
> Ask general Kovanica questions. Search existing posts first and create one post per question. Share relevant public details only; support will never need your seed phrase or private key.

### `#bug-reports` — Forum channel
**Topic to paste:**
> Report software bugs with steps to reproduce, expected and actual behavior, version, and sanitized logs. One post per bug. For tracked fixes, link or file an issue in the official project repository.

### `#security-reporting`
**Topic to paste:**
> For suspected vulnerabilities, follow the private reporting instructions here: [security contact or disclosure policy]. Do not post exploit details, credentials, private keys, or sensitive user data publicly. This channel is not monitored as an emergency hotline.

## 7. VOICE

### `Community Lounge` — Voice
**Topic to paste:**
> Informal community conversation. Keep it respectful and don’t share private or sensitive information.

### `Builder Office Hours` — Voice
**Topic to paste:**
> Scheduled technical help and builder discussion. Check the event listing for the host, topic, and time. Never share secrets while screen sharing.

### `Town Hall` — Voice / Stage
**Topic to paste:**

## Recommended permissions

- Keep `#announcements`, `#official-links`, `#network-status`, `#release-notes`, and `#roadmap` read-only for members; allow only designated publishers to post.
- Make `#rules-and-safety` and `#roles` easy to find during onboarding. Restrict role assignment to approved self-assignable roles.
- Use Forum channels for help, bug reports, network feedback, and proposals so each topic has its own title, tags, and thread.
- Give moderators only the permissions they need. Limit `@everyone` mentions, invite creation, channel management, and bot administration.
- Keep security reporting instructions visible, but route sensitive reports to a private, monitored contact rather than a public channel.
- Don’t treat Discord roles, messages, or polls as binding protocol governance unless a separately documented process says so.

- **Bug reports:** `Web` · `Wallet` · `Node` · `SDK / API` · `Needs reproduction` · `Confirmed`
- **Network feedback:** `Connectivity` · `Sync` · `Performance` · `Explorer / API` · `Suggestion`
- **Proposals:** `Discussion` · `Client-only` · `Ledger impact` · `Consensus impact` · `Decision recorded`

## Launch checklist

- Replace every bracketed link and private security contact placeholder with verified official destinations before opening the server.
- Confirm the live network and P2P details from current official sources; don’t hard-code outdated ports or endpoints into pinned messages.
- Test onboarding and channel permissions with a non-staff account.
- Enable moderation safeguards, configure a clear abuse-report route, and assign named owners for official update channels.
- Start with this structure; add channels only when recurring activity has a clear owner and purpose.
